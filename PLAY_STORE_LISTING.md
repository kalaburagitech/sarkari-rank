# Play Store listing — compliant description (Misleading Claims fix)

Paste the block below into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Full description**, then save & submit the app update. It adds (1) a clear "not a government entity" disclaimer and (2) official source links — the two things the rejection asked for.

The source URLs here match the in-app **About & Disclaimer** screen so the store and app are consistent.

---

## Full description (copy from here)

SarkariRank – Government Exam Preparation: Mock Tests, Previous Year Papers, Study Notes & Current Affairs.

This app does NOT provide government services, applications, or recruitment. Always verify official notifications, eligibility, exam dates, syllabus, admit cards, and results on the official government websites listed below.

Prepare smarter for competitive exams — SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railways, Police, State PSC, Teaching and Defence.
